**Track:** AI Enterprise Solutions

**Title:** Concord: freight documents that agree before customs

**The user and the problem**
Customs brokers and freight forwarders in Baku handling Middle Corridor shipments. Every shipment comes with an invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and air waybill, filled in by different parties. Today an employee cross-checks them by eye: 5-20 minutes per shipment (interview: Air Cargo Azerbaijan). The most frequent errors are in consignee details and in amounts, for example a 5 USD difference between line items and the invoice total. Names of the same company appear in Azerbaijani, English and Russian spellings, so simple matching fails. Concord reads all documents, cross-checks them, shows every discrepancy with the value and location from each document, and drafts the correction email to the shipper.

**Disclosure: Models**
OpenAI GPT-6 Luna (gpt-6-luna) via API: document extraction (PDF/image to JSON) and company-name matching across transliterations. All numeric and ID checks are deterministic Python code. The logo was generated with ChatGPT image generation and vectorised by us.

**Disclosure: Data**
Synthetic data only. 30 development shipments (seed 7) and [N] holdout shipments (seed 2026), generated by our script `app/data/generate.py` with injected errors and ground truth. No real customer documents. Interview notes from one industry contact (Air Cargo Azerbaijan).
